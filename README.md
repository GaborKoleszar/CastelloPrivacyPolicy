# Privacy Policy

_Effective 28 October 2026_

This privacy policy applies to the Castello app for Android devices (the "Application"), made by Gabor Koleszar (the "Service Provider").

## In short

Castello edits your videos entirely on your device. Your videos, projects and settings are never uploaded. The only information that leaves your device is what you choose to send with **Send feedback**, which needs you to sign in with a Google account.

## Your videos stay on your device

All video editing happens on your device: changing the frame rate, stabilising, AI upscaling, trimming, cropping, colour changes and joining clips into projects. The Application reads the videos you pick and saves the results to your device's storage (by default, the Movies folder). It does not upload your videos, frames, audio or any information about them.

The Application stores the following only on your device:

- Your settings, such as theme, accent colour and the last-used conversion options
- Your projects: the list of clips, their edits and a thumbnail image, plus permission to read the clips you added
- The results of a short speed test the Application runs on first start, used to estimate conversion times

This information is not sent to the Service Provider. If you use Android's backup, Android may include it in your device's backup to your Google account, under Google's terms. You can delete it at any time by clearing the Application's storage or uninstalling it.

## Artificial Intelligence

The Application uses AI models to upscale videos. The models are built into the Application and run on your device's processor. Your videos are not sent anywhere for AI processing, the models are not trained on your data, and the AI is not used to profile you, personalise content or make recommendations.

## Send feedback

Send feedback is optional. To use it, you sign in with your Google account. When you send a message, the Application stores the following with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore):

- Your Google account's email address, so the Service Provider can reply to you
- A user ID that Firebase creates for your account
- Your message (up to 255 characters)
- Your device's manufacturer and model (for example "samsung SM-S918B"), so the Service Provider can match reports to devices
- The time your message was sent, which also enforces the limit of one message per day

The Service Provider uses this information only to read and reply to your feedback and to fix and improve the Application. It is not used for advertising or marketing, it is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone except Google, which stores it on the Service Provider's behalf. It is sent over an encrypted connection.

## Information the Application does not collect

The Application has no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not track the screens you use or how long you use them, and it does not use cookies or similar tracking. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or other apps.

When you sign in or send feedback, Google's servers necessarily receive your device's IP address to make the connection. Google handles it under its own privacy policy. The Service Provider does not store it.

## Third-party services

The Application uses these Google services for Send feedback. They have their own privacy policies:

- [Google Play services](https://policies.google.com/privacy) (Sign in with Google)
- [Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## International data transfers

Google may store and process feedback data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Google uses legally recognised safeguards for these transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Legal basis (EEA and UK users)

The Service Provider processes feedback data because you choose to send it (your consent) and to answer and act on your feedback (legitimate interest in supporting and improving the Application). You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your data as described below.

## Data retention and deletion

Feedback messages are kept until you delete them, or until the Service Provider deletes them once they are no longer needed.

You can delete your data yourself in the Application at any time: open **Send feedback**, sign in if needed, and tap **Delete my data**. This deletes all your messages, your daily-limit record and your sign-in account. Signing out alone does not delete anything.

You can also ask the Service Provider to delete your data, or to tell you what data they hold about you, by emailing kgabi92@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with. The Service Provider will reply within 30 days.

Uninstalling the Application deletes everything it stored on your device, but not feedback you have already sent. Use Delete my data first if you want that deleted too.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to receive a copy of it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use these rights, contact the Service Provider at kgabi92@gmail.com.

## California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

The Service Provider does not sell or share your personal information, as these laws define those terms, and does not use it for targeted advertising. You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to have it deleted, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. The personal information collected is described under Send feedback above.

## Disclosure required by law

The Service Provider may disclose feedback data if required by law, for example to comply with a court order, or when they believe in good faith that it is necessary to protect their rights or someone's safety.

## Children

The Application is not directed at children under 16. Send feedback requires a Google account, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has sent personal information, contact the Service Provider at kgabi92@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

## Security

Feedback data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. Access rules allow each user to read and delete only their own messages, and only the Service Provider can read all messages. Everything else the Application stores stays in its private storage on your device.

## Data breach notification

If a data breach affects your personal data, the Service Provider will notify you as required by applicable law.

## Changes

The Service Provider may update this privacy policy, for example when the Application gains new features. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change means more information leaves your device, the Application will tell you before that happens.
